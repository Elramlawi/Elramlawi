## About

El Ramlawi is a family business founded in **1965** in Mansoura, Dakahlia. What started as a local shop grew into a wholesale and retail distributor of home appliances and housewares, serving households, brides-to-be and traders across Egypt.

This account hosts the **digital tools** we build to run and grow the business: our website, internal ERP, and marketing systems.
